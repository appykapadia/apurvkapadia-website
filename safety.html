Safety & Quality

Safety and quality remain our highest priorities during execution.

Every work front is managed through project-specific safety planning, crew briefing, permit discipline and inspection procedures.

Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)

Work activity risks are identified before execution and communicated to site teams.

Risk Assessment

Site conditions, utility risks, machinery movement and public interface risks are reviewed.

Toolbox Talk (TBT)

Daily TBT helps align crews on hazards, responsibilities and safe work methods.

Permit to Work Systems

Controlled execution for excavation, drilling, electrical interface and restricted work zones.

Quality Inspection

Duct alignment, depth, reinstatement, documentation and client inspection requirements are tracked.

PPE & Barricading

Helmets, reflective jackets, safety shoes, cones, signage and controlled access are enforced.
