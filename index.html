About Us

Professional contractor for industrial, road, building and utility infrastructure works.

M/S. Apurv A Kapadia is a civil and infrastructure contracting firm engaged in execution of industrial civil works, road and pavement works, building and RCC works, utility corridors, underground electrical infrastructure, HDPE duct networks, HDD crossings and allied project support.

We have project experience with leading organizations including L&T, Adani, AMNS and Reliance group projects while maintaining strict safety, quality and execution standards.
