About Us

M/S. Apurv A Kapadia

Civil and infrastructure contractor based in Surat, Gujarat, operating across India.

Contracting firm focused on industrial, road, building and utility execution.

The company is led by Mr. Apurv Arvindbhai Kapadia, an M.Tech in Construction Management, who combines modern engineering practices, project management expertise, and innovative construction methodologies to deliver efficient and reliable solutions to clients.

Leadership & Experience

At the heart of our organization is the guidance and experience of Mr. Arvindbhai B. His technical knowledge, practical wisdom, and unwavering commitment to quality form the foundation upon which our company stands today. His leadership continues to inspire our team and drive our pursuit of excellence.

Our philosophy: Every project is a commitment, every client is a partner, and every challenge is an opportunity to create lasting value.
